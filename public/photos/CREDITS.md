# Dish photos

Nine of these images are now the kitchen's own photos. The rest are still
**stand-ins**. To replace one, drop in the kitchen's own photo of the same dish,
keep the file name, and the site picks it up on the next deploy: a 4:3 JPEG
around 900x675 works best.

The bottled drinks deliberately have no photo — the brand and the size in the name
are enough, and a stock bottle shot adds nothing. That left `uje.jpg` and `cola.jpg`
attached to nothing; both files stay here, and the kitchen screen's photo field can
point an item back at either one by typing `uje` or `cola`.
